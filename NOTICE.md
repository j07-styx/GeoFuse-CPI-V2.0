## BioLiP3

BioLiP3 source data are provided by the Zhang Lab. The provider states that BioLiP is freely available for non-commercial research use. Users must follow the provider's current terms and cite the BioLiP3 resource and original source publications.

- Download and terms: https://zhanggroup.org/BioLiP/download.html
- BioLiP3 article: https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkag1141

The small prepared replay bundle contains derived, label-free feature arrays and frozen reference predictions for reproducing the reported retrospective analysis. It does not include the raw affinity table or raw structures. This repository does not grant rights beyond those available from the original source.

## ESM-2 650M

ESM-2 650M weights are not redistributed here. The inference helper downloads the official fair-esm checkpoint and verifies its SHA-256 before use.

- Model file: https://dl.fbaipublicfiles.com/fair-esm/models/esm2_t33_650M_UR50D.pt
- fair-esm project: https://github.com/facebookresearch/esm

## PerceiverCPI

PerceiverCPI is an external comparator. Its original implementation is available at https://github.com/dmis-lab/PerceiverCPI. The local reproduction revision used for the matched benchmark comparison is recorded in docs/data-provenance.md. This repository does not redistribute PerceiverCPI source or weights.
